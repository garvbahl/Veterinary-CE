Introduction to Veterinary Dentistry, Radiology and Oral Surgery

2-Day Lecture and Wet Lab Course for Veterinarians

Enhance your veterinary dentistry skills with our 2-Day Lecture and Wet Lab Course designed exclusively for veterinarians seeking to improve their proficiency in oral health assessment, radiographic interpretation, and common dental procedures. While this course covers many topics that are applicable to felines, there is a primary focus on canine dentistry. Please see our Introduction to Feline Dentistry and Oral Surgery for a feline focused lecture and wet lab.

Day 1

Lecture

  • Oral pathology and basic dental anatomy
  • The anesthetized oral exam
  • Performing, and correcting dental radiographs
  • The 12-step Comprehensive Oral Health Assessment and Treatment
  • Radiographic interpretation
  • Closed root planing and Doxirobe placement
  • Regional anesthesia

Wet Lab

  • Obtaining diagnostic radiographs utilizing canine and feline cadaver specimens
  • Oral exam/charting
  • Hand instrumentation usage and importance
  • Closed root planing, curette usage and Doxirobe gel placement
  • Practicing regional anesthesia in cadaver specimens
  • Discussion of treatment plans and navigating those conversations

 

Day 2

Lecture

  • Surgical extractions
  • Surgical flaps and closure
  • Oronasal fistulas
  • Retained root tip retrieval
  • Bonded sealant of uncomplicated crown fractures

Wet Lab

Canine cadaver specimens only will be used for the below procedures.

  • Surgical extraction of maxillary canine
  • Surgical flaps and closure
  • Oronasal fistula repair
  • Retained root tip retrieval
  • Bonded sealant of an uncomplicated crown fracture on a mandibular premolar

This class is RACE approved for 16 hours of continuing education credits.

Cancellation Policy

In the event you are unable to come to class, we strongly encourage you to find another veterinary technician or assistant to take your place.  If you must cancel, we will attempt to fill your spot. If we are able to find a replacement, we will refund your class tuition minus 10% administration fees. Note, that it can be very difficult to fill last-minute openings. If we are unable to find a replacement, we will not be able to refund your tuition.

Typically, our courses fill to capacity.  If a program is cancelled due to insufficient enrollments or unforeseen circumstances, your registration will be fully refunded.  Other expenses, including cancellation or change charges assessed by airlines, hotels, travel agencies, or other organizations cannot be refunded, this is the responsibility of the registrant.  The registrant will be notified as soon as possible if the class will be canceled.  A minimum of 6 registrations will be necessary to hold class.

We recommend that you purchase trip insurance to avoid the expense of a class cancellation. Trip insurance policies can potentially reimburse you for tuition, canceled lodging deposits, unused airplane flights, and all other trip costs you may have already incurred.
